About The Pulse

Empowering Morocco's entrepreneurial ecosystem through comprehensive data analytics and strategic insights

What is The Pulse?

The Pulse is Morocco's premier startup ecosystem analytics platform, providing comprehensive dashboards and insights for investors, entrepreneurs, and policymakers.

We deliver real-time data on funding trends, sector analysis, and growth patterns across Morocco's startup ecosystem.

Our Mission

To democratize access to startup data and foster informed decision-making that accelerates Morocco's economic transformation through innovation.

We connect startups with investors, help policymakers make data-driven decisions, and enable researchers to understand market dynamics.

Who We Are

The Pulse is developed by the Centre of Entrepreneurship at Africa Business School - UM6P, Morocco's leading academic institution driving innovation across Africa.

Our multidisciplinary team combines academic research with industry experience, bringing together data scientists, entrepreneurship experts, and economic analysts.
