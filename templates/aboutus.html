About The Pulse

The data backbone of Morocco's startup ecosystem, connecting entrepreneurs, investors, and policymakers through actionable insights.

What is The Pulse?

The Pulse is Morocco's premier startup ecosystem analytics platform. It provides comprehensive dashboards and real-time data on funding trends, sector analysis, and growth patterns across the Moroccan entrepreneurial landscape.

Designed for investors, entrepreneurs, researchers, and policymakers, the platform aggregates and structures data from hundreds of sources to paint a clear picture of where innovation is happening, how capital flows, and which sectors are emerging.

Notre Mission

  • Démocratiser l'accès aux données sur les startups marocaines — rendre l'information accessible à tous les acteurs de l'écosystème.
  • Favoriser la prise de décision éclairée — connecter startups, investisseurs, incubateurs et décideurs politiques grâce à des données fiables et actualisées.
  • Accélérer la transformation économique du Maroc par l'innovation — documenter, catalyser et amplifier la dynamique entrepreneuriale.
  • Democratize access to startup data — making information accessible to all ecosystem stakeholders.
  • Foster informed decision-making — connecting startups, investors, incubators, and policymakers through reliable and up-to-date data.
  • Accelerate Morocco's economic transformation through innovation — documenting, catalyzing and amplifying the entrepreneurial dynamic.

Qui sommes-nous

The Pulse est développé par le Centre of Entrepreneurship — Africa Business School — UM6P, l'institution académique de référence au Maroc pour l'innovation et l'entrepreneuriat en Afrique.

The Pulse is developed by the Centre of Entrepreneurship — Africa Business School — UM6P, Morocco's leading academic institution driving innovation across Africa.

Notre équipe pluridisciplinaire combine recherche académique et expérience terrain, réunissant data scientists, experts en entrepreneuriat et analystes économiques pour construire la vision la plus complète de l'écosystème startup marocain.

Our multidisciplinary team combines academic research with industry experience, bringing together data scientists, entrepreneurship experts, and economic analysts to build the most comprehensive view of the Moroccan startup ecosystem.
